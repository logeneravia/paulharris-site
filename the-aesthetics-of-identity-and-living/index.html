The aesthetics of identity and living.

This section of writing has been forming for the past couple of decades. I’m using this blog as a way to get my thinking down on paper, and to engage with you.  Since this project is about identity and change, and the influence of relationship on them, it seems appropriate to be in relationship with you, the reader.  I invite you to comment, reflect, enjoy, use, and share.  If you do share quotes or content please cite the source and link back to these pages if you’re using the web.
